# TPS AI Trading Assistant — Release 1.1

Release date: 10-08-2026

## Highlights

- Added the NSE Equity Research workspace with searchable share selection and watchlists.
- Added adaptive CE/PE paper-trade evaluation using market structure, EMA, VWAP, SuperTrend, volume, OI/PCR, VIX and support/resistance context.
- Added permanent Auto Attempt Report records with candle time, scores, confirmations and rejection reasons.
- Added automatic date-wise Roman Hindi **Post Market Analysis of TPS** reports after market close, including restart backfill and candle-wise explanations.
- Added Smart Money/price-action research for liquidity sweeps, BOS/CHOCH, fake breakouts, VWAP traps, candle patterns and volume profile context.
- Added India VIX monitoring, CAS Analysis, Stock Options Watch and limited-risk Option Strategies.
- Added the Risk Control Center with predefined NIFTY, BANKNIFTY and SENSEX option lot quantities.
- Added Next-Day Bias, About, Help and dated in-app release notes.
- Added selectable UI design systems, improved responsive cards and consistent mouse-wheel/scrollbar navigation.
- Added a complete read-only Dhan broker adapter. TPS securely stores Client ID, Dhan PIN and TOTP setup secret, then automatically generates a fresh 24-hour access token on startup and before expiry.
- Added automatic Dhan Security ID mapping for NSE/BSE indices, India VIX, equities, futures and options, with Dhan candle, quote, OI and live-price support.

## Safety

TPS remains a read-only analysis and paper-trading assistant. It does not place, modify or cancel broker orders and does not guarantee returns. Verify live prices, option liquidity, risk and news before any manual trade.
