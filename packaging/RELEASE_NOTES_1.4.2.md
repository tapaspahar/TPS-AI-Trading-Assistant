# TPS AI Trading Assistant — Release 1.4.2

Release date: 20 August 2026

## What changed

- Market session timing is editable in Settings: Pre-open, Regular Trading Start and Market Close.
- Early Watch is clearly labelled as a near-qualified completed-candle study alert, not entry approval.
- Tabs use theme-aware colours so selected and unselected pages remain readable.
- Market Snapshot uses stable clustered swing zones for support and resistance instead of replacing levels from every new 5-minute candle.
- Fast NIFTY/BANKNIFTY/SENSEX switching discards stale analysis and unrelated live ticks, preventing cross-index values and flashing cards.

TPS remains read-only decision-support and paper-validation software. Release 1.4.2 does not place, modify or cancel broker orders.
