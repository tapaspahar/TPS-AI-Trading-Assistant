# TPS AI Trading Assistant 1.4.7

Release date: 26-08-2026

- Every closed defined-risk paper strategy now appears as a separate numbered `STR` report, with its own entry, exit, result, P&L, evidence and date/period Excel export.
- Post Market Analysis now includes the Strategy Trades ledger, separate closed/open/win/loss totals and each closed strategy outcome.
- Market-close reconciliation closes any remaining paper strategy from its last verified saved mark and creates the missing session review. It never places a broker order.
- Existing settings and local production records remain unchanged across the update.
