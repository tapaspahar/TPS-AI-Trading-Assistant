# TPS AI Trading Assistant - Release 1.3

Release 1.3 begins with capital protection and recovery discipline.

- Dedicated Overtrading Protection Center.
- Persistent daily emotional-state and paper-only check-in.
- Default limit of one new paper capture per day in Recovery Mode.
- 48-hour cooling lock after two consecutive paper losses.
- Thirty separate paper sessions before real-money eligibility can be reviewed.
- One central guard for automatic and manual paper captures; scores cannot override it.
- Observation, reports and historical evidence stay available while captures are paused.

TPS remains research and paper-validation software. It does not place broker orders.
